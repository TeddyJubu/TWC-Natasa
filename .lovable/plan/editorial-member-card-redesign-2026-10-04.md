# Editorial member card redesign

## Goal
Bring the directory cards closer to the supplied tall editorial reference while retaining The Women’s Collective’s bright neighborhood palette and existing member content.

## Build
- Recompose the catalogue card into a tall, single-profile layout with a small collection label, circular catalogue number, oversized serif name, role and neighborhood line, large monochrome portrait, and a bordered business footer.
- Keep each neighborhood’s vivid color as a focused signature on the number, location, portrait mat, and footer accent.
- Add a clear arrow affordance without inventing profile-page links that do not exist.
- Preserve consistent card heights, aligned portrait starts, accessible labels, responsive typography, and color-on-hover portraits.

## Verification
- Check the directory at desktop and mobile widths for text fit, aligned rows, and overflow.
- Confirm filtering, hover/focus behavior, and the current build remain healthy.
