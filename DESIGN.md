# The Women’s Collective Design System

The Women’s Collective is warm, direct, and editorial: one self-hosted family, Archivo variable (heavy condensed headlines over a clean normal-width text face) on white grounds with near-black ink, IBM Plex Mono for labels, colour fields that always keep ink text, and outline pills. The canvas's three-star mark is the canonical logo (2026-11-26). Its seven neighborhood colors express distinct local identities while the central star represents where the community meets. Sunflower yellow appears only as a small highlight (ribbons).

## Live public site
The public pages (`/`, `/about`, `/events`, `/neighborhoods`, `/faq`, `/contact`, `/join`, `/auth` and status pages) follow the canvas design system (`docs/design-canvas/`, "01 Programme", as drawn: owner decision 2026-11-26, no deliberate differences), not the component rules below. Rollout: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-canvas-design-system-design.md` (done 2026-11-26: foundation, public pages, form pages and sign-in, signed-in pages). Those rules still govern the design-system components themselves and the `/components` showcase.
- Kit: `src/app/twc.css` (primitives: `twc-*` classes on the `sb-*` tokens), `src/app/twc-sections.css` (the canvas sections every area shares: section frame `twc-sec`/`twc-shead`/`twc-phd`, constellation bands `twc-cband` (native `<details>`), `twc-c-*` colour fields, `twc-inline` + `twc-grid6`/`twc-tile`, event rows `twc-ev`, stepped `twc-cards`, `twc-duo`/`twc-panel`, `twc-map`, `twc-toast`), one stylesheet per area (`twc-home.css`, `twc-public.css`, `twc-forms.css`, `twc-app.css`) and `src/app/layout/twc.tsx` (`Icon`, `JoinButton`); chrome is the prop-less `AppNav` (the lockup left, links and the cobalt Join pill right) and `AppFooter` (white under a thin black rule: a giant Archivo 900 "find your people." over Plex Mono columns). Reference page: `src/routes/index.tsx`, the canvas's own homepage (2026-11-26): its headline kept for screen readers only, as 01 Programme hides the hero; five constellation bands, each opening (native `<details>`) onto the canvas's folder sheet: words and Join, the star chart (one spark per roster member, hub counts, open seats where a neighborhood has none) and the sliding profile cards (open seats when there are none), lit together on hover; the member strip; the experience tiles with the canvas's stock photos (`src/assets/photography/home/CREDITS.md`); the program from Cal; the letter and partner cards; the ambassador and partner panels. The partner panel shows the canvas's price ranges.
- Type (owner decision, 2026-11-26: the canvas as drawn, superseding 2026-11-24's Archivo-only rule): Instrument Serif (self-hosted, `--font-serif`) for the display headings `twc-display`/`twc-h2`/`twc-h3`/`twc-s3` and their italic accents, in **sentence case**; Archivo variable for text, buttons, chips, nav and the heavy condensed band names and giant words (800–900, `font-stretch` 62–75%); IBM Plex Mono for labels and indexes.
- Colour (owner decision, 2026-11-26): the Programme ground `sb-ground` `#f1f4f8` behind every page, white `sb-paper` sheets and cards, `#151414` ink, muted `#525a66`, stone `sb-cream` `#e7ecf2`; the canvas paper grain on coloured cards and fields and the film grain on photos (`.twc-film`, stops after 4.5 s); the ink pill for Join; cobalt `sb-blue-ink` for inline links; wine for errors; the accents and constellation band colours as `sb-*` tokens in `styles.css`. Every colour field keeps ink text (white fails AA on sage and tomato); `src/app/contrast.test.tsx` checks the pairs.
- Shapes: the canvas pill (`twc-btn`: 1.5px border, sentence case, presses to .97; `--outline`, cobalt `--blue`, `--sm` tracked capitals), tags (`twc-chip`), dashed mono flags (`twc-flag`), pill fields with a square textarea, 34px cards with a hairline; space, radius, shadow, tilt and motion tokens are `--twc-*` in `twc.css`. The old scrapbook pieces (polaroids, stamps, scallops, ribbons, the admit ticket) are gone.
- Motion: CSS only (band folders opening, pill press, arrow nudge, toast, staggered rise, the form error shake); nothing on a public page loops, and every animation stops under reduced motion.
- Facts: a `[BRACKETED]` placeholder on a board is an unknown fact — drop the element rather than invent one. No stand-in photos: members without a portrait get an initials mat; neighborhoods without a photo get a colour + logo tile. Stock photography lives in `src/assets/photography/site/` with `CREDITS.md`.

### Events and booking (public)
`/events`, `/events/<slug>`, `/book`, `/book/<type>` and `/booking/<uid>` use the canvas kit (programme rows, the ink-ruled calendar grid, category colour fields); their pieces are in `src/features/cal/` (`events-ui`, `calendar`, `rsvp`, `booking-ui`, `ui`).
- Filters are `twc-choice` pills with `aria-pressed`; the tab, view, category, month and search live in the URL.
- A card with no https cover, or one that can't be loaded, gets a date tile in its category colour (one of eight ink-checked fields, picked by a hash of the category name; stone without one), never a stand-in photo. Descriptions are plain text with their line breaks.
- The month calendar shows each event on its own local day, starts on the visitor's first weekday, and has one tab stop: arrows, Home/End and PageUp/PageDown move, Tab reaches that day's events. Phones show a count per day that opens the day's list.
- Every RSVP and booking outcome is announced in a live region. Times always name their zone.

## Setup
- Import `src/design-system/styles/theme.css` right after `@import "tailwindcss";`.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `src/design-system/assets/fonts` (Archivo variable, Latin + Latin Extended + Latin italic, SIL OFL, from `@fontsource-variable/archivo` 5.3.0) and loaded by the canonical theme file; the root preloads the Latin file.
- Dark mode: add `dark` to `<html>`. Any block can go dark locally with `Section tone="night"` or the `tone-night` class — all tokens invert inside it.
- Import from the barrel only.

## Hard rules
- Token utilities only (`bg-canvas`, `bg-surface`, `text-ink`, `text-ink-muted`, `text-ink-faint`, `bg-sky`, `bg-sun`, `border-line`). No raw hex or Tailwind palette colors. The exceptions are colors that are fixed rather than themed:
  - Plain `white` and `black`, with opacity where needed: type, rules and glass over photography and night chrome; black scrims behind type on photos; black type on bright neighborhood and accent fills where white fails contrast; the fixed fills of the `white` button and `solid` badge; the black contact-sheet panel.
  - Neighborhood colors are data (hex in the `neighborhoods` table, with a hex fallback list in `src/features/neighborhoods`) and reach app UI only through `--hood`. The theme's neighborhood tokens (`bg-lic`, …) are for the constellation and specimens.
  - `CircularGallery`'s WebGL text colors are plain values, because canvas text can't read CSS tokens.
- Archivo for everything: headings, big numbers and wordmarks heavy and condensed (800 at 75% width); text, buttons and badges at normal width, 400–600. No serif faces and no cream grounds.
- Every public section opens with a `Badge` above a centered `Heading` (use `SectionHeader`).
- Public pages start with `Hero` (photo, glass badge, white headline left, short copy right, thin white rule, glass info card + big stat).
- Rhythm: light sections → `tone="dome"` black panel → `tone="night"` continuation → light again. Don't scatter random greys.
- Buttons have 12px corners (`rounded-control`), not pills; badges are pills. Cards use `rounded-card`.
- Yellow (`sun`) only for ribbons/small highlights, never large fills or body text.
- Forms follow an editorial zine language: numbered sections, fine rules, heavy condensed emphasis, and asymmetrical multi-column compositions. Use `FormSection` to group long applications.
- Use `editorial` fields on feature forms and `boxed` fields in compact utility contexts. Never mix both field variants within one form.
- Mint, lilac, and signal orange are supporting editorial accents for selected states and specimen panels; keep them subordinate to canvas, ink, and photography. Neighborhood tokens are reserved for constellation and location-led storytelling.
- No inline styles for design decisions; vary via `variant`/`size` props. Two exceptions: (1) data-driven neighborhood color arrives as one inline CSS variable `--hood` (via `MemberCard`'s `tone` prop, or a `hoodStyle()` helper in app code) and is consumed only through the static utilities `bg-(--hood)`, `text-(--hood)`, and `border-(--hood)`; (2) styling computed at runtime that no utility can express: `BorderGlow`'s gradients, masks, blend and glow, `ProximityText`'s per-letter transforms, the `Tabs` grid sized by its item count, the mobile menu's body scroll lock, the member card's name, sized by its longest word (`--name-chars`), and the `/account` map's neighborhood ring and scale bar, sized by its zoom (`--ring`, `--scale`).
- Motion is editorial and purposeful: shared reveal, stagger, panel, hover, and feedback patterns only. Never add decorative motion that competes with content. Anything that moves on its own stops within 5 s (`CurvedLoop`) or offers a pause control (`Ribbon`).
- Use `Reveal` for viewport entry and `Stagger` for short sequenced groups; both preserve content and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Long applications use `FormProgress`, `FormSection`, `ChoiceGroup`, `FormNotice`, and `FormActions`; conditional follow-ups reveal directly beneath their controlling choice.
- Member directories use the `MemberCard` catalogue variant in a dense black contact-sheet panel; each card carries its neighborhood color, passed in through the `tone` prop, as a quiet signature (header rule, catalogue number, neighborhood label, and portrait mat), and portraits begin monochrome to reveal color on hover. Members without a portrait show their initials on a neutral mat, never a stand-in photo. Give cards `onOpen` and render one `MemberDialog` per page to open a member's full profile.

## Internal tools (the canvas working views)
Every signed-in page (`/account`, `/directory`, `/feed`, `/admin`, `/admin/forms`, and any new page behind sign-in) uses the canvas design system's working views ("01 Programme", `docs/design-canvas/`; sub-project 4 of `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-26-canvas-design-system-design.md`), on the same terms as the public site: Archivo only, white `#fff` ground, `#111` ink, no cream, no paper grain. `.air` sits on the page root next to `app-ground`. The `air-*` class and token names are historical (the airmail look they once drew — letters, stamps, perforations, envelopes, chevron stripes, postmarks, tickets, red mono numbers — is gone); keeping the names kept the swap visual only. Internal pages never use the public page rhythm (bands, the site header and footer), and public pages never use `.air`.
- Tokens (`theme.css`, `--bl-air-*` → `@theme inline`): `air-paper` (white), `air-navy` (the ink `#111`: text, rules, switches, primary pills, the current nav item), `air-muted` (canvas muted `#5c5751`), `air-red` (wine `#93313a`: destructive buttons, refusals, errors — `.air` points `danger` at it), `air-tint` (stone `#e7ecf2`: notices, reply threads, sub-panels), `air-sky` (a lighter stone: hovers), `air-rule` (hairline `#d9d9d9`), `air-line` (stone rule `#c8d0da`: row dividers); radii are 0 (sections, cells, menus and dialogs are square) and shadows flat, except `shadow-air-menu`: a 1.5px ink edge and the canvas's hard 6px ink shadow, on menus, dialogs and the save bar. App tokens: `app-pending` band butter, `app-ok` lime, `app-accent` cobalt (links and focus), `app-gel` lime / `app-gel-green` mint (a form's open / always-on pill, ink text). `.air` re-points `ink`, `ink-muted`, `app`, `app-sunken` (stone), `app-muted`, `app-line`, `app-rule`, `danger` and the kit's `sb-*` at them, so shared controls and older `app-*` utilities follow without forks. Inside a colour field (`twc-c-*`, `pass--*`) muted text and links turn ink, the canvas rule. Every text pair is in the contrast test.
- Type: Archivo 800 condensed for titles, big numbers and names (`font: 800 condensed` in the kit; `.font-display` inside `.air` gets it too), Archivo at normal width for everything else, IBM Plex Mono for small tracked labels (`air-lbl`, section indexes, `twc-label`/`twc-eyebrow`/`twc-index` inside `.air`). Badges and filter pills are tracked Archivo capitals, buttons sentence case.
- Kit (`src/app/layout/app-ui.tsx`, `app-classes.ts`, the `air-*` classes in `src/app/twc-app.css`): `Letter` (a section under a hairline ink rule: a muted mono index "01" — it numbers itself in page order without `no` — a heavy condensed title, then any body), `Card` (a `Letter` with a second rule whose children, or a list's items, sit flush on hairlines; a notice or a filled sub-panel keeps its inset), `Row`, `Field` (a label/value cell), `Chip` (the canvas badge: butter = waiting on someone, ink = settled, wine = a no, outline = neutral; the gel tones are a form's status pill), `Toggle`, `EditButton`, `InlineError`, `EmptySlot` (the canvas's dashed lock: a dashed ink box with an icon), `TicketDate` (a stone date block; lime when confirmed), `Stamp` (a member as the canvas's directory card: a 4:5 portrait or her initials on lime, never a stand-in photo, a number badge, her name and a caption; a toggle with `pressed`, framed in ink while chosen, else it opens something). Controls: `mainButton` (the page's one primary action, a full-size ink pill), `dangerButton` (wine, for what can't be undone), `primaryButton` (small ink pill), `secondaryButton` (small outline pill), `fieldClass` (the canvas pill field; a textarea stays square), `linkClass` (cobalt, underlined). Pills press to .97.
- Chrome: the admin pages use `AppShell`: a white sidebar ruled off in ink with the brand, then the canvas's leader nav — Home, Inbox, People, Calendar and Forms on hairlines (a butter count where something waits), the current one in ink with a 2px bar — and Admin activity and Sign out at the foot; the same nav in a ui-sdk Drawer below lg; a `TopBar` that is the canvas page head (the title as a heavy condensed `h1` over a hairline ink rule, who is signed in at its end, sticky from lg); an area's pages are underline tabs under it. `/account`, `/feed` and `/directory` use `AirShell` (below).
- Layout: admin page column `max-w-250`, gutters `px-4` on phones and `sm:px-8`; sections are separated by space and a hairline, never boxed. Nothing scrolls sideways at 375px except a table inside its own region.
- Motion: colour transitions, the .97 press, the switch knob, the save bar rising into place, the Love pop and the map pin's three beats; all off under reduced motion.
- Forms (`/admin/forms`): a form is a box in a 1px ink frame with its answer count in a foot under a rule (`Ticket` in `panels.tsx`), lifting onto a hard ink shadow on hover; New form is a dashed ink box. Home's tiles are colour fields (lilac, blush, lime, butter) with ink text. A custom form's Questions tab is the form in a 1.5px ink frame: every word is edited in place, with no box until hover or focus, and choices are the ink-outlined pills visitors tap.

## Accessibility
- White text on photos needs the built-in gradients (`bg-sky-fade`, card overlays); don't remove them.
- `text-ink-faint` only on large text (24px+; 3:1 on canvas) — fading statements and idle tabs; never small or essential body copy. Placeholders use `ink-muted` at 85%.
- Primary button fills use `bg-sky-strong` (white text 4.6:1); `sky` stays for hero sky, rings and accents.
- Keep focus rings; wrap inputs in `FormField`.
- Associate hints and errors with their controls using `aria-describedby`; mark invalid controls with `aria-invalid`.

## Voice
Warm, direct, and full of personality. Speak like a neighbor inviting someone into a conversation. Celebrate women’s work, make introductions generously, and give members a reason to participate. Keep headlines short: Title Case inside design-system components, sentence case on the live public site.
