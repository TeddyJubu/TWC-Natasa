# The Women’s Collective Design System

The Women’s Collective is warm, direct, and editorial: full-bleed sky photography, an elegant serif (Gilda Display) over a clean sans (Inter), soft grey pill badges, and dramatic shifts into pure black "night" sections with a domed top edge. The uploaded halftone constellation is the canonical logo. Its seven neighborhood colors express distinct local identities while the central star represents where the community meets. Sunflower yellow appears only as a small highlight (ribbons).

## Live public site (scrapbook)
The public pages (`/`, `/about`, `/members`, `/members/:slug`, `/gatherings`, `/neighborhoods`, `/faq`, `/contact`, `/join`, `/auth` and status pages) follow the scrapbook design canvas, not the component rules below. Those rules still govern the design-system components themselves and the `/components` showcase.
- Kit: `src/app/twc.css` (`twc-*` classes on the `sb-*` tokens) and `src/app/layout/twc.tsx`; chrome is the prop-less `AppNav`/`AppFooter`. Reference page: `src/routes/index.tsx`.
- Type: Gilda Display for headings in **sentence case** (never bold), a lowercase Caveat line above many of them (`twc-eyebrow-script`), Instrument Serif italic for stamp and value titles, Geist for body, Fragment Mono for indexes and ticket meta.
- Colour: paper `#fcfcfa` ground; cream, sun, night, mint and lilac bands joined by `Scallop` dividers; stamp cards in olive, mustard, rose, shell and terracotta; neighborhood colours only through `--hood`. Small blue text is `sb-blue-ink`; `sb-blue` only at 24px and up.
- Shapes: pill buttons and chips (`twc-btn`, `twc-chip`), polaroids, arched and disc photos, wavy-edged stamps, the admit-ticket CTA, 6px cards and fields.
- Motion: CSS only, every looping animation has a pause control (`TwcRibbon`, the home page's pause button) and stops under reduced motion.
- Facts: a `[BRACKETED]` placeholder on a board is an unknown fact — drop the element rather than invent one. No stand-in photos: members without a portrait get an initials mat; neighborhoods without a photo get a colour + logo tile. Stock photography lives in `src/assets/photography/site/` with `CREDITS.md`.

## Setup
- Import `src/design-system/styles/theme.css` right after `@import "tailwindcss";`.
- Fonts are self-hosted in `src/design-system/assets/fonts` and loaded by the canonical theme file.
- Dark mode: add `dark` to `<html>`. Any block can go dark locally with `Section tone="night"` or the `tone-night` class — all tokens invert inside it.
- Import from the barrel only.

## Hard rules
- Token utilities only (`bg-canvas`, `bg-surface`, `text-ink`, `text-ink-muted`, `text-ink-faint`, `bg-sky`, `bg-sun`, `border-line`). No raw hex or Tailwind palette colors. The exceptions are colors that are fixed rather than themed:
  - Plain `white` and `black`, with opacity where needed: type, rules and glass over photography and night chrome; black scrims behind type on photos; black type on bright neighborhood and accent fills where white fails contrast; the fixed fills of the `white` button and `solid` badge; the black contact-sheet panel.
  - Neighborhood colors are data (hex in the `neighborhoods` table, with a hex fallback list in `src/features/neighborhoods`) and reach app UI only through `--hood`. The theme's neighborhood tokens (`bg-lic`, …) are for the constellation and specimens.
  - `CircularGallery`'s WebGL text colors are plain values, because canvas text can't read CSS tokens.
- Serif for headings, big numbers and wordmarks; never bold it — weight 400 only. Sans for everything else.
- Every public section opens with a `Badge` above a centered serif `Heading` (use `SectionHeader`).
- Public pages start with `Hero` (photo, glass badge, white headline left, short copy right, thin white rule, glass info card + big stat).
- Rhythm: light sections → `tone="dome"` black panel → `tone="night"` continuation → light again. Don't scatter random greys.
- Buttons have 12px corners (`rounded-control`), not pills; badges are pills. Cards use `rounded-card`.
- Yellow (`sun`) only for ribbons/small highlights, never large fills or body text.
- Forms follow an editorial zine language: numbered sections, fine rules, serif emphasis, and asymmetrical multi-column compositions. Use `FormSection` to group long applications.
- Use `editorial` fields on feature forms and `boxed` fields in compact utility contexts. Never mix both field variants within one form.
- Mint, lilac, and signal orange are supporting editorial accents for selected states and specimen panels; keep them subordinate to canvas, ink, and photography. Neighborhood tokens are reserved for constellation and location-led storytelling.
- No inline styles for design decisions; vary via `variant`/`size` props. Two exceptions: (1) data-driven neighborhood color arrives as one inline CSS variable `--hood` (via `MemberCard`'s `tone` prop, or a `hoodStyle()` helper in app code) and is consumed only through the static utilities `bg-(--hood)`, `text-(--hood)`, and `border-(--hood)`; (2) styling computed at runtime that no utility can express: `BorderGlow`'s gradients, masks, blend and glow, `ProximityText`'s per-letter transforms, the `Tabs` grid sized by its item count, the mobile menu's body scroll lock, and the `/account` map's neighborhood ring and scale bar, sized by its zoom (`--ring`, `--scale`).
- Motion is editorial and purposeful: shared reveal, stagger, panel, hover, and feedback patterns only. Never add decorative motion that competes with content. Anything that moves on its own stops within 5 s (`CurvedLoop`) or offers a pause control (`Ribbon`).
- Use `Reveal` for viewport entry and `Stagger` for short sequenced groups; both preserve content and honor reduced-motion preferences.
- Long applications use `FormProgress`, `FormSection`, `ChoiceGroup`, `FormNotice`, and `FormActions`; conditional follow-ups reveal directly beneath their controlling choice.
- Member directories use the `MemberCard` catalogue variant in a dense black contact-sheet panel; each card carries its neighborhood color, passed in through the `tone` prop, as a quiet signature (header rule, catalogue number, neighborhood label, and portrait mat), and portraits begin monochrome to reveal color on hover. Members without a portrait show their initials on a neutral mat, never a stand-in photo. Give cards `onOpen` and render one `MemberDialog` per page to open a member's full profile.

## Internal tools (airmail)
Every signed-in page (`/account`, `/admin`, `/admin/forms`, and any new page behind sign-in) is one design system: airmail letters on an onionskin desk, ported from the Doop canvas "Account · Variation B — Airmail". `.air` sits on the page root next to `app-ground` (which points the display, script and editorial faces at Geist). Internal pages never use the public page rhythm (no bands, scallops, ribbons, polaroid heroes or the site header/footer), and public pages never use `.air`.
- Tokens (`theme.css`, `--bl-air-*` → `@theme inline`): colour `air-sky` (the desk, hovers), `air-paper` (letters, sidebar, top bar), `air-navy` (ink, rules, switches, the current nav item, settled chips), `air-red` (card numbers, stamps, the page's one primary button), `air-muted` (labels, secondary text), `air-rule` (hairlines), `air-line` (row dividers, cells, dashed slots), `air-tint` (a navy wash for notices and wide cells); radius `rounded-air-card` (14px letters and chrome), `rounded-air-cell` (10px forms, notices, the map), `rounded-air-menu` (18px menus); shadow `shadow-air-card`, `shadow-air-menu`, `shadow-air-lift`. Light only. `.air` re-points `ink`, `ink-muted`, `app`, `app-sunken`, `app-muted`, `app-accent`, `app-line`, `app-rule` and the kit's `sb-*` at them, so shared controls and older `app-*` utilities follow without forks. Every text pair, including text on the tint, is in the contrast test.
- Type: Gilda Display for titles, big numbers and ticket dates (`font-gilda`; `h1` and card `h2` bold by request, Gilda has one weight so the browser thickens it, 400 everywhere else), Geist for everything else, IBM Plex Mono for small tracked capitals: labels (`air-lbl`, and `twc-label`/`twc-eyebrow`/`twc-index` inside `.air`), card numbers, chips, stamps. Self-hosted in `src/assets/fonts/airmail/`. Sentence case for buttons and copy.
- Kit (`src/app/layout/app-ui.tsx`, `app-classes.ts`, `air-*` in `twc.css`): `Stamp` (a member as a perforated postage stamp; a toggle with `pressed`, else it opens something), `Letter` (a numbered letter, "Nº 01" in red mono — it numbers itself in page order without `no` — a Gilda title, then any body), `Card` (a `Letter` with a navy rule whose children, or a list's items, sit flush on ruled lines; a notice or a filled sub-panel keeps its inset), `Row` (mono label / value), `Field` (a customs-form cell), `Chip` (mono capitals: outlined = still open, navy = settled, red = no, ring = neutral; the forms keep their gel pills), `Toggle`, `EditButton`, `InlineError`, `EmptySlot` (a dashed slot with a blank stamp), `TicketDate`. Controls: `redButton` (the page's one primary action), `primaryButton` (navy pill), `secondaryButton` (outlined pill), `fieldClass`, `linkClass`.
- Chrome: the admin pages use `AppShell`: a paper sidebar (`air-panel`) with the brand in Gilda, mono group labels (My account, then Admin and Forms for admins — UX only), a navy pill for the current section, Sign out at the foot; the same nav in a modal `<dialog>` drawer below lg; a sticky paper `TopBar` (the page title as a bold Gilda `h1`, who is signed in) as wide as the card column. `/account` uses `AirShell` (below). The desk carries the airmail chevron stripe at its top.
- Layout: page column `max-w-250`, gutters `px-4` on phones and `sm:px-8`; letters stack with `gap-6`. Nothing scrolls sideways at 375px except a table inside its own region.
- Motion: colour transitions, the switch knob, the button hover lift and the save bar rising into place, all off under reduced motion.
- Forms (`/admin/forms`): a form is a ticket (`Ticket` in `panels.tsx`: paper card, dashed tear, a punched notch at each end): a card on the list with its answer count on the stub, and the summary at the top of its own page. A custom form's Questions tab is the form on a paper sheet: every word is edited in place, with no box until hover or focus, and choices are the pills visitors tap.

## Member profile (`/account`)
The member's own page on the internal design system: the envelope cover, stamps, rubber stamps and tickets. Account-only pieces live in `src/features/account/` (`AirShell`, `Locked`, the cards).
- Plain answers: the Visibility card's subtitle says in one sentence who can see her card with the switches as they stand, and links her public page once it is saved and live; the bio field counts its characters.
- States: before approval, Gatherings, Headshot session and Introductions show the locked panel ("Unlocks once you're welcomed in"), linking to `/join` only when she has no application; headshot requests show cream / mint / rose notices for requested / confirmed / declined.
- Chrome: `AirShell`, with no sidebar. A top bar holds the brand, then her avatar, name and number as the account menu's trigger (with a chevron), plus Sign out. The menu opens on hover with a mouse, and on click, Enter or tap otherwise; Escape, a click outside or picking an item closes it. It is a paper panel listing the account sections (navy when current, `aria-current` while that card is in view), and admins also get Admin to `/admin`.
- Cover: an envelope with a chevron border holding her member pass. Her portrait is the stamp (click to upload; the camera badge always shows), her catalogue number its value, business and "<neighborhood> post" on its band. The postmark is dated the day she joined. A verified seal (or her application status), her name as the `h1`, her Specialty in serif, role · business, then the pass fields: neighborhood, number, joined, headshot and next gathering she's going to. Each field shows only when it is set; nothing is invented. Then a decorative barcode and "View public card".
- Cards: numbered letters ("Nº 01" in red mono, Gilda Display title, a navy rule). Public card is a customs form (three columns from lg) with the business logo row. In your words is a letter on ruled lines. Membership has a rubber "Approved" stamp and the big catalogue number. Visibility has switches flush on rules. Headshot session and Gatherings are tickets with a dashed date stub (red when confirmed, navy otherwise). Empty and locked cards are a dashed slot with a blank stamp. From lg it is a 12-column bento: Public card full width, then 7/5 pairs, then Gatherings (tickets two across) and Introductions full width. A customs form in a half-width letter stays one column. Introductions are the directory's stamps (`Stamp` in the kit): two across on phones, one strip of up to four from sm (each at most 190px), opening `MemberDialog`; the sheet has no ground of its own, so one introduction is one stamp.
- Where you belong (right under the cover): her pin on a MapLibre map of OpenFreeMap's Positron tiles, greyed and multiplied onionskin blue. The pin sits on the neighborhood's centre (a central subway stop from `src/features/neighborhoods/places.ts`, never an address), with a dashed ring for its walkable reach, a postmark with the ZIP, her popup and a navy neighborhood chip. The map is a picture with no drag or scroll zoom, so it never traps the page's scroll; buttons zoom, re-centre and go full screen. Beside it is the "home post": the neighborhood name, "Queens, New York" and the ZIP, coordinates, nearest stop with line bullets, where it gathers (next gathering there) and neighbors here (from Introductions, "4+" at most), and a privacy note. A neighborhood without a place entry gets the post column only; no neighborhood gets a dashed slot that opens the Public card's picker. The pin's pulse beats three times, then stops (none under reduced motion). On phones the chip moves to the top-left and the controls to the bottom-right.
- Member pass ("View public card", and the save bar's Preview): a native modal `<dialog>` holding her pass, `MemberPass.tsx` and the `pass-*` classes. It is a die-cut postage pass: her portrait (or her initials in Gilda, never a stand-in photo), a "Verified member" tab once approved, a postmark dated the day she joined, a stamp with her number and "<neighborhood> post", a perforation with notches at the edge, then her name in Geist capitals, role, business and only the facts that are set, plus the barcode. Portrait below 880px, landscape from there. Ink is fixed `#1d1c1a`. The pass colour is hers, from the kit's mustard, rose, lilac and mint (`profiles.card_color`, migration 0012, saved the moment she picks a swatch; shown only on `/account` for now). A fine pointer tilts it and lights a star foil; there is no motion on touch or under reduced motion.
- Save bar: paper with an airmail edge, sticky at the foot, only while there is something to say: "Unsaved changes" with Discard, Preview and Save (red); "Saving…"; a failed save; and "All changes saved" for a moment after one. Leaving with unsaved edits asks first.

## Member directory (`/directory`)
The signed-in directory on the internal design system: `AirShell` (its account menu adds "Member directory" and links the account sections back to `/account`), a sheet of stamps and the torn-out pass. `noindex`; the public `/members` keeps the scrapbook site.
- Intro: "Members" as a giant bold Gilda `h1`, a lede, and stats (members, neighborhoods, the month printed) over a dashed rule. Then neighborhood filters (`air-filter`, navy when pressed, with counts; only neighborhoods someone is in) and a search box that folds accents (`matchesSearch`).
- Sheet (`air-sheet`, `air-slot`): twelve stamps a sheet, 2 / 3 / 4 across at phone / sm / xl, on white with `--air-perf-edge` so neighbours share their holes. Each is a toggle button: number in red mono, value in cents, portrait (or initials on navy, never a stand-in), name in Geist capitals, a caption, "twc." on its side. Torn out, it lifts and tilts off the sheet and leaves a dashed "Torn out · tap to return" spot; no idle motion, none under reduced motion.
- Pass (`air-dir-pass`): sticky beside the sheet from lg, above it below (tearing scrolls it into view). Only the facts she published: number, name, role and business, neighborhood, craft, her words, who she wants to meet; "Open her page" (red) and her website. A dashed perforation and a decorative barcode. Empty, it says how to tear one out.
- Audience: approved members and admins see the directory; anyone else signed in sees the public cards with a notice.

## Newsfeed (`/feed`)
The members' feed on the internal design system: `AirShell` (its account menu adds "Newsfeed"), `noindex`. Feed-only pieces live in `src/features/feed/` (`Composer`, `FeedPost`); classes are `air-feed-*`, `air-entry-*` (a post), `air-ms` (a mini stamp), `air-compose-*`, `air-kind(s)`, `air-act`, `air-thread`, `air-reply*`.
- Layout: one column on phones (the feed, then her pass, then the side column); the side column beside the feed from lg; her pass on the left from xl (228 / feed / 264).
- Masthead: "<weekday, month day> · Queens edition" in mono, "The feed" as a giant bold Gilda `h1`, "N new this week" with a red dot, over a dashed rule.
- Composer: a paper letter with her mini stamp and a borderless field (an outline on focus), "Posting to every member of the collective", then a dashed rule over the kinds (native radios as pills, navy when chosen), a photo button (up to four, each with an optional description that becomes its alt text) and Post, the page's one red button.
- Filters: All posts, her neighborhood (only when she has one), Asks & offers, Gatherings (`air-filter`). Newest first, 20 a page, "Older posts", then "You're all caught up".
- Post: a paper letter. Her mini stamp (a perforated portrait, or initials on navy, never a stand-in), her name (linked to her member page when she has one), role · business · neighborhood · when (`postedAgo`: minutes, hours, then New York days), the kind as a ring `Chip`, and a trash button for her own (or any, for admins) that confirms first. Words keep their line breaks; photos sit one wide, two side by side, or three and four in a grid, each opening full size, the first carrying a white postmark of her neighborhood and the day. "You, Cara and 21 others loved this" names only who loved it. Love (a heart, red when pressed) and Reply sit on a rule; replies are a navy wash (the latest two until "Show earlier replies"), and Reply opens a field with a round navy send button.
- Side: her pass in the colour she picked on `/account` (`pass--*`, fixed ink): her number, name in Geist capitals, craft · neighborhood, a dashed perforation, her posts and loves received, a decorative barcode; then "Keep it neighborly". Neighbors to meet: her introductions as a pair of stamps, each opening `MemberDialog`. Coming up: the next four gatherings on navy date tiles, red with "You're going" when she is.
- States: before approval the feed is a dashed slot ("Unlocks once you're welcomed in", `/join` only without an application); loading and errors use the kit's status line and `InlineError`; an empty feed or filter says so in a dashed slot.
- Kept out on purpose: messages, saved posts, notifications, following neighborhoods, an ask's quantity/budget box and a ticket on gathering posts (no data behind them yet; gatherings stay admin-made events).

## Accessibility
- White text on photos needs the built-in gradients (`bg-sky-fade`, card overlays); don't remove them.
- `text-ink-faint` only on large text (24px+; 3:1 on canvas) — fading statements and idle tabs; never small or essential body copy. Placeholders use `ink-muted` at 85%.
- Primary button fills use `bg-sky-strong` (white text 4.6:1); `sky` stays for hero sky, rings and accents.
- Keep focus rings; wrap inputs in `FormField`.
- Associate hints and errors with their controls using `aria-describedby`; mark invalid controls with `aria-invalid`.

## Voice
Warm, direct, and full of personality. Speak like a neighbor inviting someone into a conversation. Celebrate women’s work, make introductions generously, and give members a reason to participate. Keep headlines short: Title Case inside design-system components, sentence case on the live public site.
